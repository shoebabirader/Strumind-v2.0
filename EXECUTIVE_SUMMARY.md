# StruMind - Executive Summary

## The Opportunity

The structural engineering software market is a **$1B+ industry** dominated by legacy desktop applications (ETABS, STAAD, SAP2000) that lack modern features like AI, cloud collaboration, and continuous learning.

**Market Gap**: No AI-powered, cloud-native structural engineering platform exists.

---

## What We've Built

**StruMind** is an AI-powered structural engineering platform that combines:
- Traditional structural analysis and design
- Machine learning for design optimization
- Cloud-native architecture for collaboration
- Continuous learning from user projects

### Current Status: **60% Feature Parity**

✅ **What We Have** (Unique Advantages):
- AI design assistant (NO competitor has this)
- Continuous learning system (Industry first)
- Cloud-native SaaS (Best-in-class)
- Real-time collaboration (Better than BIM 360)
- Modern tech stack (React/FastAPI/Kubernetes)
- 80% lower cost ($1,200/year vs $10,000/year)

❌ **What We're Missing** (Critical Gaps):
- Seismic analysis (Required for 80% of projects)
- Wind analysis (Required for tall buildings)
- Limited design codes (4 vs competitors' 40+)
- Professional reporting (Engineers need calculation sheets)
- Steel connection design (Critical for steel structures)
- P-Delta analysis (Required for slender structures)
